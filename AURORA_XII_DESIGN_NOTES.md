# AURORA XII — Design notes

## Direction
Task-first, mobile-first, progressive disclosure, calm visual hierarchy.

## Primary jobs
1. Decide what to do.
2. Use Terrain in seconds.
3. Diagnose a photograph.
4. Experiment visually.
5. Retrieve the underlying corpus.

## UX principles
- One primary action per surface.
- Progressive disclosure instead of dense dashboards.
- Persistent mobile navigation for the five most frequent tasks.
- Secondary content remains in the drawer/library.
- Touch targets are >= 48 px where practical.
- Reduced-motion support.
- The Book remains a searchable knowledge layer, not the primary navigation model.

## Research references
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines: navigation and touch-first interaction.
- Adobe Lightroom Learn: quick actions and masking on mobile.
- Mobile exploratory visualization research: discoverability, rapid in-context exploration, graceful recovery.
- Photography Mapped / camera simulators: direct manipulation to make camera relationships visible.
